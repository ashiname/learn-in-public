You can make callout blocks in obsidian, but actually they can be collapsible blocks too, not just in HTML, but in markdown.

You can make it like a regular callout, but you add either a `+` or a `-`
```
> [!info]+ Information
> Lorem Ipsum dolor.
> Sit amet
```

You can choose whether it is naturally collapsed or not, by choosing between the symbols `+` or `-` after the `[!info]` brackets.
Use `+` for naturally opened, or use `-` for naturally collapsed.

Result:

> [!info]+ Information
> Lorem Ipsum dolor.
> Sit amet
