You get this link:

```
http://img.youtube.com/vi/VIDEOID/maxresdefault.jpg
```

And then you replace `VIDEOID` with the actual id.

Example:

in the video: `https://youtu.be/I1hLZ2OchZ8`, you copy the part after `watch?v=`, in this case: `I1hLZ2OchZ8`

```
http://img.youtube.com/vi/I1hLZ2OchZ8/maxresdefault.jpg
```

if nothing appears, try changing `maxresdefault` to either `mqdefault` or `default`, standing for full resolution, medium res or low res.
